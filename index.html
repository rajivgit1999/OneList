Privacy Policy for OneList

Effective Date: October 24, 2023
Last Updated: October 24, 2023

This Privacy Policy describes how OneList ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the OneList mobile application (the "Application"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

OneList is designed to function strictly as an on-device local list management application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Personal Data

OneList does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, IP address, or device identifiers.

Application Data (List Content)

Any items, titles, or quantities you add to OneList are stored locally on your device using an internal Android database (Room Database).

  • This data is stored strictly on your local device.
  • We do not have access to your stored list items.
  • No list data is transmitted to external servers or third-party cloud services.

2. Permissions

OneList is designed to operate with minimal permissions:

  • The application does not request internet access (android.permission.INTERNET).
  • The application does not request sensitive permissions such as location, camera, contacts, microphone, or external storage access.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

OneList does not integrate any third-party services, including:

  • Advertising networks (e.g., AdMob).
  • Analytics services (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics).
  • External tracking technologies or cookies.

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • All list data remains stored inside the Application's private sandboxed local storage on your device.
  • You can delete individual list items or remove all stored list items at any time directly within the app interface.
  • Clearing the Application data via Android System Settings or uninstalling OneList will permanently delete all stored list items from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

OneList does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is requested or collected, the application is designed for users of all ages.

6. Security

We value your trust in using OneList.

Because all your data remains exclusively on your device within Android's app sandbox, your data is protected by standard Android operating system security controls.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
